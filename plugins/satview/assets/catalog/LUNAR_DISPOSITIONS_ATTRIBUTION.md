# Lunar Disposition Overlay

`lunar_dispositions.csv` contains curated corrections for objects which remain
tagged as undecayed Moon orbiters in CelesTrak SATCAT after a primary source has
confirmed that they landed or impacted the Moon.

The overlay is deliberately small and evidence-based. It does not infer a
disposition merely from age or loss of contact.

Sources:

- Chang'e-4 surface landing: China National Space Administration,
  https://www.cnsa.gov.cn/english/n6465652/n6465653/c6805049/content.html
- HAKUTO-R Mission 2 / RESILIENCE impact: NASA Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter,
  https://www.nasa.gov/missions/lro/nasas-lro-views-ispace-hakuto-r-mission-2-moon-lander-impact-site/
