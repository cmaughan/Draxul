# Lunar Surface Objects

`lunar_surface_objects.csv` is generated by
`scripts/build_satview_lunar_surface_catalog.py`. Runtime loading is entirely
offline.

Coordinates and feature metadata come from the Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter
Camera team's *Anthropogenic Features on the Moon* product in the IAU Moon 2000
Geographic Coordinate System:

- https://data.lroc.im-ldi.com/lroc/view_rdr/SHAPEFILE_ANTHROPOGENIC_OBJECTS
- Wagner, R. V., Nelson, D. M., Plescia, J. B., Robinson, M. S., Speyerer,
  E. J., and Mazarico, E. (2017), *Coordinates of anthropogenic features on the
  Moon*, Icarus 283, 92-103.

Mission identity, COSPAR/JCAT identifiers, owner, and country fields are
enriched from Jonathan McDowell's General Catalog of Artificial Space Objects
(GCAT), licensed under CC BY 4.0:

- https://planet4589.org/space/gcat/root.html
- https://planet4589.org/space/gcat/tsv/cat/landercat.tsv

LROC coordinates take precedence over GCAT coordinates. Exact source versions,
hashes, and reviewed conflicts are recorded in `lunar_surface_sources.json`.
